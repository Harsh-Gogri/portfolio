## Overview

Collaboration Groups enables users to share their digital assets with contacts through organized groups with role-based permissions. Users can create groups with up to two admins and assign three additional roles (Manager, Editor, Viewer) with specific access levels.

The feature offers flexible sharing options — real-time, terminally ill, or posthumous sharing — allowing users to control when their shared assets become accessible to group members.

This desktop redesign focused on simplifying the complex permission system, improving visual clarity, and creating a seamless experience that integrates with Mitt Arv's Asset Vault.

## Challenge

<CalloutBox type="problem">
  The existing Collaboration interface created significant friction in what should be a straightforward sharing process. The UI was confusing and cluttered, making it difficult for users to understand the permission structure and manage their groups effectively.
</CalloutBox>

**Complex Group Creation Process:**
The workflow for creating collaboration groups felt unnecessarily complicated with multiple steps that weren't clearly indicated. Users had to navigate through unclear paths just to set up basic sharing permissions.

**No Upfront Role Visibility:**
When users landed on the collaboration dashboard, there was no immediate information about their assigned roles or what restrictions applied to them. This lack of transparency forced users to discover limitations through trial and error rather than seeing their permissions upfront.

<ImageGallery images={[
  { src: "https://cdn.prod.website-files.com/6772e66ddfd2c58201e2c6c9/68fd24b40ddfca807025ec4b_Collaboration%20-%20Dashboard.jpeg", alt: "Collaboration Dashboard (Before)", caption: "Original dashboard with no upfront role visibility." }
]} />

**Viewport Clutter and Poor Space Utilization:**
When opening a collaboration group, both the main collaboration dashboard and the group dashboard stacked horizontally, creating cramped, competing sections. Editing roles and access settings triggered small modals that had nowhere to breathe in an already cluttered viewport.

The top portion of the screen remained empty while valuable space was wasted, and the persistent "Create a new contact" panel occupied premium real estate where it wasn't contextually necessary — reducing the space available for core collaboration workflows.

]} />

**Responsive Breakdown:**
These space utilization problems were compounded by poor responsiveness. On smaller viewports, the already cluttered interface became nearly unusable, with inaccessible controls making the sensitive task of managing and sharing assets frustrating and error-prone.

## Approach

The strategy centered on creating a consistent, intuitive experience that aligned with Mitt Arv's other features while dramatically simplifying the collaboration workflow.

**Design System Consistency:**
Visual and interaction patterns established in the Emotional Will and Asset Vault features were maintained, creating a cohesive product experience. Users familiar with one feature could immediately understand how to navigate Collaboration Groups.
