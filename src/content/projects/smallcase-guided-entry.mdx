## Overview

Smallcase is a thematic investment platform that allows users to invest in curated portfolios of stocks and ETFs.

This independent product case study explores how to improve activation rates for new and passive investors who struggle with decision paralysis when faced with too many investment options.

## Research & Discovery

To understand why investors hesitate or avoid using investment platforms, I conducted primary research combining qualitative interviews and quantitative validation.

### User Interviews

I spoke with 3 early-career professionals (20-25 minute sessions each) with varying investment exposure - SIP investors, stock investors, and crypto-first investors. These conversations revealed behavioral patterns and emotional barriers that numbers alone wouldn't capture.

### Key Behavioral Patterns

- Most started investing after their first salary
- Investments were heavily influenced or managed by trusted people (family/friends)
- Strong preference for low-effort, long-term investing (SIPs, "set and forget")
- Light tracking behavior - users mostly checked total value/profit occasionally

### Decision-Making Patterns

- Strong reliance on external guidance from family members or friends with investing knowledge
- Low confidence in choosing funds/stocks independently
- When guidance disappeared, investment activity slowed or stopped entirely

> I stopped investing because I didn't feel confident making decisions on my own once my brother got busy.

### Survey Validation

To validate whether interview insights represented broader patterns, I conducted a survey with 13 respondents - mixed investors, with the majority being SIP users.

### What This Confirmed

The survey validated all interview patterns - investors prefer low-effort approaches, have moderate to low decision confidence, and find choice overload genuinely stressful. Guidance and simplicity were strongly desired across the board.

## Problem Definition

Based on research insights, I identified several interconnected problems contributing to activation hesitation.

### User Need

> When I want to invest my money, I want clear, trustworthy guidance, so that I can confidently choose without spending hours researching.

### Core Pain Points

- **Confusion About Where to Invest:** Users faced with hundreds of smallcases had no clear starting point or filtering mechanism aligned to their situation.
- **Low Confidence in Making Decisions:** Only 7.7% felt very confident. Most depended on trusted advisors - when that disappeared, so did their activity.
- **Dependency on External Guidance:** 75% relied on friends/family, but this wasn't sustainable or scalable for consistent investing.
- **Research Feeling Heavy:** Users wanted to learn but found self-directed research time-consuming and mentally overwhelming.

{/* Image: SWOT Analysis */}

### Problem Prioritization

Through RICE scoring, I evaluated three hypothesized problems:

- **P1:** Event-driven investors struggle with insufficient funds at final checkout (Medium confidence)
- **P2:** New explorers struggle choosing first investment from wide range of options (High confidence)
- **P3:** Portfolio optimizers get irrelevant suggestions from generic benchmarks (Medium confidence)

### P2 became P0

Addressing choice overload and lack of guidance had the highest potential impact on activation.

## Solution Exploration

I explored multiple solution directions to address the core problem of decision confusion and choice overload.

- **Smallcase-Specific Referrals:** Allow existing investors to refer specific smallcases to friends/family with milestone-based rewards
- **Contextual Personalized Suggestions:** Collect user inputs and recommend best-fit smallcases with guided roadmap
- **Ratings & Reviews:** Let investors rate and review smallcases for social validation
- **Reduce Visible Choices:** Show only top 3-5 smallcases to prevent overwhelm
- **Explain "Why Recommended":** Show simple reasoning for each recommendation
- **Smart Onboarding Quiz:** Quick quiz to map users to best-fit smallcases

### Prioritization (RICE)

- **Smallcase-Specific Referrals:** Reach: 2, Impact: 3, Confidence: 2, Effort: 2 = RICE Score: 6
- **Guided Entry System:** Reach: 3, Impact: 3, Confidence: 3, Effort: 3 = RICE Score: 9

The Guided Entry System scored highest because it directly solved the root cause - users needed structured decision support, not just social validation or fewer random options.

## Designs

The Guided Entry System introduces a structured onboarding flow that transforms Smallcase from an open marketplace into a guided decision platform.

### Solution Overview

Entry trigger → 5-step quiz (less than 60 seconds) → Profile summary → Personalized roadmap → Invest or edit preferences
