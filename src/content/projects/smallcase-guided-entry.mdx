Smallcase has helped millions of investors discover curated portfolios built around themes, strategies, and long-term investment ideas. While users showed strong intent when exploring smallcases, a significant number dropped off before completing their first investment.

Research revealed that users were not abandoning because they lacked interest—they were abandoning because they lacked confidence. Concepts like broker connection, order execution, portfolio minimums, and basket investing created uncertainty during critical moments of the journey.

The Guided Entry System was designed to reduce friction, build confidence, and help first-time investors successfully complete their first investment without feeling overwhelmed.

## Problem

<CalloutBox type="problem">
  Users were highly motivated to invest but lacked confidence in the process. The onboarding journey assumed prior knowledge of investing workflows, causing uncertainty and drop-offs before investment completion.
</CalloutBox>

For experienced investors, the investment flow felt straightforward. For first-time users, however, every step introduced new questions:

- What exactly is a Smallcase?
- Why do I need to connect a broker?
- Is my money safe?
- What happens after I click Invest?
- Am I making a mistake?

The more uncertainty users experienced, the less likely they were to complete their first investment.

<CalloutBox type="insight">
  Users rarely abandoned because they disliked the product. Most drop-offs occurred when users encountered unfamiliar financial concepts without enough context to make a confident decision.
</CalloutBox>

Key observations from research:

- Users understood stocks better than basket investing.
- Broker connection was perceived as a high-risk action.
- Long flows increased decision fatigue.
- Users wanted reassurance before committing money.
- Many users struggled to understand what would happen after investment.

## Approach

We redesigned the onboarding experience around progressive guidance instead of progressive disclosure alone. Every step needed to answer the user's most important question before they asked it.

## Solution

### Guided Education

Instead of immediately pushing users toward investment actions, the experience first established a clear understanding of what Smallcase is and how basket investing works.

Educational content was integrated into the flow rather than isolated inside help sections, ensuring users could learn without leaving their investment journey.

### Broker Connection Guidance

Broker connection represented one of the largest trust barriers.

The redesigned experience introduced contextual explanations that clarified:

- Why a broker is required
- What permissions are being granted
- What data remains protected
- What happens after successful connection

This reduced uncertainty while preserving flow momentum.

### Confidence Before Commitment

Rather than focusing solely on transaction completion, the system emphasized investment confidence.

Key information was surfaced before critical actions, helping users understand:

- What they were investing in
- Why the portfolio existed
- Expected investment behavior
- Next steps after purchase

## Outcome

The Guided Entry System transformed onboarding from a transactional process into a confidence-building experience.

By addressing uncertainty at each stage of the journey, users were better equipped to understand basket investing, trust the platform, and complete their first investment.

The project demonstrated that increasing user confidence—not simply reducing steps—was the most effective way to improve conversion and long-term engagement.

---

## Note

<CalloutBox type="note">
  The most valuable learning from this project was that onboarding friction was not caused by complexity alone. It was caused by uncertainty. When users understood what was happening and why, they were significantly more likely to complete their investment journey.
</CalloutBox>
